Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Alkule is an offline Pular · French · English dictionary published by Gandal ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Alkule app on iOS or Android.

Summary

Alkule does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. App data is stored locally, subject to your device's backup and transfer settings.

Data stored on your device

To work, the app keeps the following locally on your device:

  • Words you save as favorites
  • Your recent searches
  • Quiz progress (which words you have practiced and when to review them), daily streaks, and achievements
  • Display preferences, such as whether to show the ADLaM script

Gandal cannot access this data. Your operating system may include it in backups or device-to-device transfers, depending on your settings and the platform's policies. You can remove the current local data in Settings → Clear offline data, or by uninstalling the app. Manage any existing backups through your device's backup settings.

Network access

The dictionary and fonts are bundled with the app, so it works completely offline. Alkule does not make network requests, use analytics or advertising SDKs, track you, or require an account.

When you use the system share sheet to share a word, the text you share is handled by the app you choose to share it with, under that app's own privacy policy.

This website and support email

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. This website logging is separate from the offline app.

If you email support, we use the email address and message you send to respond to your request.

Children

Alkule is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
