Support

Questions, corrections to a dictionary entry, or a bug to report? Email technologies.gandal@gmail.com. Corrections from Pular speakers are especially welcome.

Does Alkule need an internet connection?

No. The whole dictionary is on your device and works offline.

Can I search without typing ɓ, ɗ, ŋ, ñ or ƴ?

Yes. Searching "buri" also finds "ɓuri".

Why are some noun classes and plurals marked with *?

The source dictionary doesn't list them, so they were suggested automatically and haven't been verified by a Pular speaker yet. If you spot a mistake, please tell us.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings → Clear offline data. Your favorites, history and quiz progress are removed from the device.

Where does the dictionary come from?

From the Webonary Pular dictionary by Oumar Bah. English translations were machine-translated from the French.
